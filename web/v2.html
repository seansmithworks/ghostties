07 Still Ghostty

Still Ghostty

Your config, themes and keybinds survive. Upstream credit paid properly, in public.

  • Your ~/.config/ghostty/config loads unchanged — Ghostties is a fork, not a rewrite.
  • Themes, keybinds and font settings all carry over.
  • The terminal engine (Zig + Metal) is upstream ghostty-org/ghostty, untouched.
  • The sidebar is an additional surface, not a replacement — Ghostties users are still Ghostty users.
08 First 60 seconds

First 60 Seconds

What actually happens after install — where it puts things, what it touches, how to leave.

  1. Install places the app in /Applications and nothing else — placeholder, confirm exact scope.
  2. First launch reads your existing Ghostty config, if one exists — placeholder.
  3. The sidebar opens via a keyboard shortcut you can change or ignore — placeholder.
  4. Uninstalling removes the app and its own preferences, leaves your Ghostty config alone — placeholder.
