03 Still Ghostty

Still Ghostty

Ghostties is a fork of ghostty-org/ghostty, not a rewrite — your config, themes and keybinds load unchanged, the terminal engine underneath is still upstream Ghostty, and the sidebar is an additional surface, not a replacement.
