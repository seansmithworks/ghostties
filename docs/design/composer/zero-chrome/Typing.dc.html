Grammar is untouched by this direction. Tab still inserts a segment plus a space; only a user-typed > arms a branch. Zero-chrome changes the surface, never the parse.
Fork to settle: centred vs. left-aligned at the caret. Centring is prettier at rest, but with no card the rows lose their shared left edge — the one alignment cue that used to say "these belong together." Left-aligning the whole stack to the caret restores grouping for free.
