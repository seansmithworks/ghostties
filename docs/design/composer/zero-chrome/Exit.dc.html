Motion — the two exits
Commit lifts. Dismiss does not. That 6px is the whole distinction.
With a card, Return and Escape could look identical — the card sliding away was the event, and what happened next was somebody else's problem. With nothing on screen but text, the exit is the only chance to say whether anything was launched.

Both exits keep blur trailing text. The text is gone before the backdrop finishes resolving, in both cases — that ordering is what makes the terminal feel like it was underneath the whole time rather than replacing something. It is also the cheapest part of this whole direction to build: two withAnimation blocks where there are currently none.
