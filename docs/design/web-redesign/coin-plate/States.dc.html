Four states, and none of them is a colour change
The backlight carries the mood; the words carry the meaning. Rest and armed differ in three ways a screenshot in greyscale would still show — the label text, the credit digit, and whether the coin is in the slot. That is the WCAG 1.4.11 argument, and it is also just better signage.

Armed
Steady bloom, no breathing. Label swaps to PRESS / ESC TO / EXIT — same three-line block, so nothing moves. Coin is gone: it is in the slot. CR 1, digit brightens.
Why PRESS / ESC TO / EXIT and not just EXIT. Keeping three lines of the same lengths means the armed swap costs zero reflow and the eye lands in the same place. It also mirrors the rest copy, so the plate reads as one sign with two messages rather than two different signs.
The credit line is not in the reference. It is the build's own addition and the only gold element on the plate — a functional readout, not signage. Keep it dim; it should be findable, never a third thing competing with 25¢ and PLAY.
