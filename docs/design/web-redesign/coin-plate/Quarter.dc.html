The coin
A quarter, with Sean on the obverse
Right-facing profile bust, the way a real quarter carries one. Deliberately low-resolution — abstract enough to be a mark, not a portrait. Four features do all the work: the swept-back crown, the glasses bar breaking the profile line, the beard mass, and the collar. Everything else is noise at this size.
12 × 12
A silhouette and nothing more. Reads as a head, not as anyone. Too far.
16 × 16 recommended
The glasses bar and the beard both survive. Drops into the field at COIN_PX 2 for the same 32px footprint as today.
20 × 20
Brow, nose and lip separate properly. Starts to look like a portrait rather than a mark — and it needs 40px on the page to hold up.
Metal
Gold — today's --coin-gold. Warmest thing on the page, so it never loses the dark field or the red plate.
Silver — what a quarter actually is. Truer, and it goes grey against the ghosts, which are also pale.
Gold is the recommendation despite being wrong for a quarter: this coin has to be spotted while drifting across a dark field full of pale ghosts, and silver is the one colour already taken.
Actual size
32px — drifting
16px — in slot
At 16px the bust is a texture, not a face — and that is fine. It only has to be legible while it drifts, and it is gone once it is in the slot.
