Parts
1
Translucent housing
Red acrylic lit from inside, not a painted frame. Brightest at the edges and at three hot spots — top centre, right flank, left flank. Falls to near-black at the base.
2
Mould seams
A vertical seam left of the panel and a horizontal one near the base. Nearly invisible, and the single strongest cue that this is a moulded part rather than a rectangle.
3
Screw bosses ×4
Moulded circular bosses at the corners, lit top-left, shadowed bottom-right. Sunk into the plastic, so they read darker than the housing around them.
4
Slot channel
Left quarter of the plate, running 78% of its height inside a lit collar. Core is near-black; both lips carry a hard catch-light, the left brighter than the right.
5
Recessed panel
Near-black, set behind the housing face. A thin lit line around its edge is the acrylic catching light — a border, but an emitted one.
6
Lamp bar
A bulb sits above the panel, out of frame. Its bleed occupies the top quarter and is what makes 25¢ blow out. This is the only part that breathes.
7
Type stack, centred
25¢ hard against the top, the three label lines pushed to the base, dead air between them. Centred, not left-aligned — the shipped build left-aligns beside the slot, which is the main deviation this canvas corrects.
Three deltas from what ships today
Aspect is 0.72, not 0.64 — 92×144 becomes 104×144 or 74×103.
Type is centred in the panel, not a left-aligned column beside the slot.
The slot gets a lit collar; today it is a bare cutout.
