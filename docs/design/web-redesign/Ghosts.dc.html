why visor won — five faces × three domes, decided pair highlighted
The size ladder is the real test. A face that only works at 260px is not a face, it is an illustration. The sidebar renders these at 12 and 16px, the favicon at 32, the social card at 200.
what carried over from round 2
  • Solid dome. The old set cut eye holes out of the silhouette, which is literally the 1980 sprite.
  • A grounding shade on the bottom rows and a top-left highlight, so they have form.
  • Personality from dome shape and skirt rhythm, not from copying named characters.
what the robot pass adds
  • An antenna as the agent tell — the one part no arcade ghost has.
  • Readout face: four status blocks instead of eyes. The most on-product of the five.
  • Panel seams read as machine at 48px and vanish cleanly below 20px.
decided
  • Face: visor. One lit slit, and it is the only one of the five that reads as a screen rather than an eyeball.
  • Dome: tall. Locked alongside the visor, not just today's dial default — the matrix's highlighted cell is the decision, not a suggestion.
  • Pupils track the cursor. Because the visor's lit block is a screen and not an eyeball, tracking it reads as a UI readout, not the Pac-Man tell it would be on dual-lens or single-lens.
