Sidebar vnext — motion spec

Strawman for Sean · branch feat/sidebar-d @ 092267f7b · 2026-10-09 · builds on the Flow 05 canvas frame (uTvGd)

Pushed, not dragged. The sidebar arrives fast, leaves faster, and only moves the thing you touched. The one moment that carries the identity is the group card narrowing into the rail column, with the glyphs riding inside it.

paneldrawer cubic-bezier(.32,.72,0,1)close cubic-bezier(.23,1,.32,1)Flow 05's values, unchanged: 260 collapse · 240 expand · 180 close · 240 reopen. AppKit drives the width and SwiftUI follows on the same curve, so they land on the same frame.
snap.spring(response: 0.24, dampingFraction: 1.0)Every layout reflow: accordion, chevron, rows in and out, drag gap, group card height. No bounce, and it keeps its velocity when you click again.
pop.spring(response: 0.32, dampingFraction: 0.8)One use only: a glyph turning into ?. It is the only overshoot in the sidebar.
fadeease-out cubic-bezier(.23,1,.32,1)Durations 80 · 100 · 120 · 150 ms. An exit is never slower than its entrance. Reduce Motion always gets a 120ms opacity crossfade, never nothing.

What changes most

  • The morph becomes one object. Today two complete lists crossfade, so glyphs and cards show double mid-flight. Proposed, the card narrows into the column and the glyphs slide 22pt inward with it.
  • Presses stop disappearing. A 250ms guard silently drops a second ⌘S that lands while the 180–260ms transition is still running. Proposed: a 60ms key-repeat guard, and the panel retargets from wherever it is.
  • Expanded labels land at about 310ms, not 750ms. Today each label waits for the full 240ms panel, plus 60ms, plus 40ms per row, with no cap.
  • Hard cuts get one honest move. Glyph state swaps, rows arriving and leaving, and selection now each have a defined transition. Tab switches and row hover stay instant on purpose.

Stays still, on purpose

  • Projects ↔ Sessions tab switch stays instant, today and proposed. It's only reachable by keyboard and menu (⌘⇧1 / ⌘⇧2), and keyboard actions don't animate.
  • Row hover tint stays instant both ways. You see it tens of times a minute.
  • Rail auto-scroll on keyboard selection becomes instant instead of easeInOut 200 (SidebarRailView.swift:248). The keyboard rule wins.
  • The popover moving between rows stays an instant jump. A 52pt glide on every row would trail the pointer.
  • The spinner keeps a fixed 80ms frame step with no easing. Under Reduce Motion it's a static ….
  • Colour and theme changes never blur or travel.
  • Terracotta never animates in the sidebar. It is reserved for waiting.

DialKit dials worth exposing

  • Snap response (0.24, range 0.16–0.40) and Snap damping (1.0, range 0.7–1.0) cover every reflow at once.

  • Animation speed (1×, 0.25×, debug only) multiplies every duration and spring response so you can watch the real app in slow motion.

Inconsistencies

  • Five vocabularies are in use: Flow 05 béziers, built-in .easeInOut(0.2), .easeInOut(0.15), spring(0.2, 1.0) and CA .easeOut. D18's sidebarCollapse is .easeIn, which the rules forbid. It's used by the composer and triage, not this sidebar.
  • The debounce contradicts the code's own comment that a second toggle "redirects smoothly". It never reaches the animator.
  • The expand stagger is uncapped. It is 40ms per row on top of 300ms, so a 10-row list lands its last label at about 0.78s.
  • Two trees crossfade instead of one morph, so the glyphs, tiles and group card are drawn twice at partial opacity mid-flight. Inferred from the code. A frame capture would confirm it.
  • widthModel.width is written to the target at t=0 while AppKit animates the hosting view's width. The content is laid out at the final width from the first frame. Inferred, not captured.
  • The row-radius tween (6 → 12) only applies to the wide selected card. Grouped rows in D use the chip, so it rarely shows.
  • Several Reduce Motion paths animate nothing: the tray axis, the accordion, the view-mode width, the browser and the composer. Reduce Motion should be gentler, not empty.
  • The popover's exit is as long as its entrance (140 / 140) and has no curve set.
  • The drag gap uses scale 0.01. It's harmless only because the spacer is clear.

Two calls that are yours

1. Morph: one object (recommended) or a tuned crossfade. One object means the group card narrows into the column and the glyphs slide 22pt with it. That's a structural change to how the sidebar hosts its two layouts, about one PR. A tuned crossfade keeps today's two-list fade with the new label timing, as token swaps only. It's cheaper, but you'll still see doubled glyphs mid-flight. Compare Today and Proposed in section 1 at 0.25×.

2. Should "needs you" pop? Recommended: yes, a pop spring with about 20% bounce, and the only overshoot anywhere in the sidebar. It's the one state that wants your eyes. The alternative is the same 150ms blur-scale swap every other glyph uses. That's calmer, but it's easy to miss in peripheral vision.
