v3 Hero Storyboard recut v2

ghostties.org · 2026-08-23 · Pre-production — nothing recorded yet

Press a key, a new agent thread is born, and you watch it take its place in the sidebar. 14 seconds, five beats, one continuous take of the real app. Plays once on load, then rests on beat 01 — the drifting ghost field owns continuous motion, so the video never loops.

What changed from v1. The first cut spent 5.5 of 14 seconds pushed into a transcript nobody is meant to read. This one makes the sidebar the subject — it opens the film and closes it — and the composer the verb. The transcript stays in frame as texture and is never zoomed.

What each thing is for

  • The SIDEBAR is the subject. It opens the film and it closes it.
  • The COMPOSER is the verb — the thing being done, keyboard-first.
  • The TRANSCRIPT is texture. It must be alive and never ask to be read. No beat centres on it, no zoom targets it.

Camera path

Sidebar, composer, sidebar. The camera leaves the sidebar only for the six seconds the composer is open, and the pull-back at 8.5s arrives on the sidebar exactly as the new row lands.

Still open

  • Beat 05's idle->working flip may happen naturally when a session starts. If it does not, it is the only beat still needing fixture help — everything else in this recut is user-driven.
  • The composer's Cmd-T binding was not located in SessionComposerOverlay.swift; confirm the actual trigger before the take.
  • The take must be fully keyboard-doable. ⌘T, type, arrow, Return. If any step needs a mouse — the breadcrumb chip has no keyboard route — it is not filmed and it is not claimed.
  • Record after beta.24 ships, not before. Re-recording means redoing both themes.
  • Frames are dark theme only. Light is an identical second take, same timings, same keystrokes.
