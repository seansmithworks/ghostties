At-the-desk capture checklist

Every step is Sean, at the machine. Screen-capture permission is granted to the terminal, not the app, and can deny silently even when visibly on.

  1. Set the scene by hand: sessions, projects, mode, per shot.
  2. screencapture -D 2 from a real Terminal window.
  3. Save reference-site captures to images/marketing/, named to match the asset table.
  4. App icon: export 1024×1024 from the existing source in images/ — no new capture.
  5. Correction: anything web-bound (hero, sidebar close-up if reused on ghostties.org) saves to web/assets/, not images/marketing/ — and ships as its own PR, separate from the reference-site work, since merging to main deploys production. Verify by reading the deployed asset after merge, not the deploy status: /assets/* carries a 1-hour cache, so a warm browser shows stale for up to an hour.
